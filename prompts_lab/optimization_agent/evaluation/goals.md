# Podcast Quality Goals

Evaluate each episode as spoken audio, not as an essay. A strong episode should feel clear, accurate, paced, and worth listening to all the way through.

## Narrative Quality
The episode has a coherent story arc with stakes, turns, examples, and a satisfying ending. It avoids a flat encyclopedia structure.

## Technical Depth
The episode explains mechanisms and trade-offs with enough detail to teach a curious listener something real. It should avoid hand-wavy summaries.

## Speakability
The script sounds natural when read aloud. Sentences are human-length, dialogue or monologue feels performable, and transitions are easy to follow.

## Engagement
The episode sustains attention through tension, surprise, vivid examples, callbacks, and changes in energy.

## Factual Accuracy
The episode uses concrete details responsibly and does not overstate uncertain claims. Claims should be internally consistent and plausible.

## Format Fit
The episode should fit its intended format. Two-host episodes should use natural back-and-forth. Solo episodes should speak directly to the listener without fake co-host dynamics.
